## 2. Strategic Insights

### Multi-Channel Attribution Discovery
- 100 customers touched **15.1 campaigns on average** before converting
- Max campaigns touched: 22 (shows high-touch B2B nature)
- **Implication:** Success requires coordinated, persistent campaigns, not single-channel pushes

### Customer Quality Paradox
- Campaigns with lowest support burden ≠ highest MRR
- "Webinar Series Q1" has 13.7 tickets/customer but still profitable
- "Security Certification" has 14.5 tickets/customer but exceptional ROI
- **Implication:** Support burden reflects product maturity, not campaign quality

### Seasonal Opportunity
- **Peak:** June (Month 6) with $201,292 revenue
- **Trough:** April (Month 4) with $111,055 revenue
- **Variance:** 81% swing between peak and trough
- **Action:** Reallocate 25% more budget to Q2 peak season

### Channel Concentration Risk
- **organic_search** dominates: 26 campaigns (52%), 767 conversions (50% of total)
- **twitter** secondary: 9 campaigns (18%), 290 conversions (19% of total)
- Remaining 7 channels share 32% of campaigns, 31% of conversions
- **Risk:** Over-reliance on 2 channels; test new channels with 10% of budget
