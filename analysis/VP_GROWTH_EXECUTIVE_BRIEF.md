## The 7 Biggest Opportunities (Ranked by Impact)

### 🎯 #1: Fix Attribution Model [Impact: Clarify $1-2M in spend efficiency]

**Problem:**
- Organic Search: -84% ROI on $6.1M spend
- Twitter: -89% ROI on $2.9M spend
- Content: -90% ROI on $3.6M spend
- **Yet business is profitable with 360% overall ROI**

**Root Cause:**
- Last-touch attribution credits "organic/direct" for brand-driven conversions
- Multi-touch journeys (15.1 touches avg) not captured
- Long sales cycles mean campaign impact shows 90+ days later

**Fix:**
- Implement 90-day attribution window (not 30-day)
- Use first-touch, last-touch, and weighted multi-touch models
- Tag all campaigns with UTM parameters for full tracking

**Value:** Correctly allocate $5-10M in future budget. Could 2-3x ROI.
