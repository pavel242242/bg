Key Insights

  • White-collar work is most vulnerable: Programmers, designers, and digital creators face the highest replacement rates as AI agents master creative and technical tasks.
  • Communication roles are next: Email readers and agile coaches are being automated as AI handles routine communication and facilitation.
  • Physical labor remains safe: Lumber jacks and hands-on professions requiring physical presence show minimal AI replacement.
  • Teaching is resilient: Despite advances in AI tutors, human teachers remain irreplaceable for holistic education.
