But Not All Jobs Are Equal

Interestingly, the data reveals a fascinating pattern: jobs requiring physical presence in unpredictable environments remain largely immune to AI replacement. Lumberjacks, for instance, show only a 3% replacement rate, as AI still struggles with the complex sensorimotor challenges of forest work.

Similarly, teachers maintain relatively low replacement rates at 28%, despite the proliferation of educational AI. Researchers attribute this to the irreplaceable human elements of emotional support, mentorship, and adaptive classroom management.

Perhaps most surprising is the 89% replacement rate among "Talkative Grooming Attendees" – professionals who previously spent hours in meetings discussing project minutiae. AI agents have efficiently taken over these roles, leading to an unexpected side benefit: a 47% reduction in corporate meeting times globally.
